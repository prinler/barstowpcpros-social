## Identity
- **What it is (one sentence):** A one-person, local tech-help business in Barstow that fixes computers, Wi-Fi, printers and security cameras, builds websites that bring in calls, and sets up practical AI for small businesses. Help is remote first, and Steve comes to you when it needs hands-on work.
- **Category:** Local IT support, small-business websites, practical AI
- **Location:** Home-based in the county outside Barstow, CA 92311. No storefront. Mobile on-site plus remote.
- **Service area:** Barstow, Lenwood, Daggett, Yermo, Hinkley, Newberry Springs, Fort Irwin, and nearby High Desert communities. Remote support outside the area.

## Positioning
- **What's different:** Not a call center and not a big-box counter. A local person who explains what's going on, fixes what matters, and tells you what it costs up front.
- **Why believe it:** 20+ years in IT. Prices are posted publicly. Remote-first means no waiting for a drive. Steve gives honest advice when something isn't worth fixing.
- **Point of view:**
  1. Tech help should be explained in plain English, with no jargon and no judgment.
  2. AI is for the busywork, and a human stays in charge. You review and approve anything a customer sees.
  3. A website should bring in calls, not just sit there. And you should own it: your domain, your accounts.
- **Won't say:** AI hype ("replace your staff", "fully automated"). Fear-selling about hackers. Anything that implies the most expensive fix is the right one.

## Audience
_Split posts about evenly between the two segments._
### Segment 1 — High Desert small businesses (owner-operators)
- **Who:** Trades, shops, offices and service businesses in Barstow and the High Desert, usually with no IT person
- **Cares about:** Getting more calls, losing less time to paperwork and repeat questions, keeping Wi-Fi, printers and cameras working
- **Believes / objects:** "Websites are expensive and don't do anything." "AI is hype, or it's scary." "I'll just deal with it."
- **Where:** Facebook (local groups, scrolling between jobs), Google Business Profile
- **Sophistication:** Beginner to intermediate
- **Transformation:** Duct-taped tech and invisible online → a site that brings in calls, systems that just work, hours back each week

### Segment 2 — Local households
- **Who:** Barstow-area residents, many older, plus military families near Fort Irwin
- **Cares about:** A slow PC, Wi-Fi that keeps dropping, email they can't get into, "I clicked something and now I'm worried", cameras that stopped recording
- **Believes / objects:** Embarrassed to ask, worried about being upsold, wary of scams
- **Where:** Facebook mostly; Instagram skews younger
- **Sophistication:** Beginner
- **Buyer vs follower note:** Adult children often follow and share on behalf of their parents.

## Voice
- **Dimensions:** Formality 2/5 · Warmth 4/5 · Authority 3/5 · Energy 3/5 · Humor 2/5 (dry, relatable) · Polish 3/5
- **We sound ___, never ___:**
  - patient, never condescending
  - plain-spoken, never jargon-y
  - straight about price, never salesy
  - like a neighbor who knows tech, never like a call center
  - practical about AI, never hyped
- **Words we use:** plain English · straight pricing · remote help first · we come to you · the High Desert · "describe it your way" · "no jargon, no judgment" · busywork · "you review, you approve" · "earn the call" · "not worth fixing"
- **Words we ban:** solutions (as a noun) · cutting-edge · revolutionize · game-changer · leverage · synergy · seamless · industry-leading / best in town · "AI-powered" as filler · "Dear valued customer" · scare words (hackers are coming!, act now!)
- **How we handle hard things:** Name the price and what's extra. Say plainly when a repair isn't worth it. Never knock a named competitor. If a problem was Steve's mistake, own it.
- **Signature devices:** Open with the customer's own words ("The printer says it's offline."). Short sentences. Problem → what it usually is → what you can try → when to call. Customer-quote humor ("'It's doing the thing again' is a perfectly good place to start.").
- **Point of view on the page:** "We" as the business, matching the website. Steve can appear by name in behind-the-scenes posts ("Steve was out in Hinkley today…").
- **Voice by platform:** Facebook: a bit longer and conversational, phone number in the post. Instagram: shorter caption, visual-first, contact info lives in the bio.
- **Pronunciation guide:** Barstow (BAR-stoh). Daggett (DAG-it). Yermo (YUR-moh). Hinkley (HINK-lee).

## Operational defaults (tool skills read these)
- **AI & synthetic media policy:** AI-generated graphics and illustrations are OK, labeled "AI-generated image" in the caption or alt text. Real jobs use real photos. Never AI-generate customers, testimonials, before/after results, or Steve. No AI avatars or voice clones.
- **Accessibility defaults:** Alt text on every image (plain description). Captions on all video. High-contrast text on graphics.
- **Visual identity:** "Desert Dusk" palette from the shield logo (theme color #0a2a30). Assets in Website/assets/brand. → design-and-templates owns colors and type.

## Guardrails
- **Security / privacy (hard rules):** Never show a customer's screen, email, address, passwords, camera footage, or home or business layout without permission. Blur serial numbers and license keys. Never ask anyone to share passwords in comments or DMs.
- **Scam-safety line:** "Barstow PC Pros will never call you out of the blue asking for remote access." Use it in any remote-support or scam-awareness post.
- **Competitor rules:** Never name or knock local competitors or big-box stores. "Not a big-box counter" is as far as it goes.
- **Sensitivity:** Scam victims get empathy, never "you should have known."
- **Topics to avoid:** Politics, and local controversy outside tech. (Suggested default; Steve to confirm.)
